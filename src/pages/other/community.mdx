# DevGuard Community

DevGuard is an open-source project built together with its community. We hold a **Monthly Open Community Call** on the last Thursday of each month — open to everyone who uses, contributes to, or is curious about DevGuard.

## Monthly Open Community Call

**When:** Last Thursday of each month, 17:00 UTC+2

<CalendarDownloadButton />

The call covers:

- Status updates on current releases and ongoing development
- Discussion of open issues and community priorities
- Feature requests and roadmap planning
- Q&A and open discussion

Everyone is welcome — whether you are a current user, evaluating DevGuard, or interested in contributing.

**Join the call:** [DevGuard Open Community Call room](https://meet.mailbox.org/room/dad9052b-7b28-40c8-bf6c-462798a88827?invite=1b3e44cc-2e46-4050-8359-bee002d8bbfe)

The agenda and notes for each call are published in the [DevGuard GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/l3montree-dev/devguard/discussions/2203).

## Community Channels

- **Matrix:** [#devguard:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#devguard:matrix.org) — the primary async community space for questions, feedback, and discussion
- **GitHub Discussions:** [github.com/l3montree-dev/devguard/discussions](https://github.com/l3montree-dev/devguard/discussions) — for longer-form conversations, proposals, and call agendas
